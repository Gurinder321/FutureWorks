Source: "FW ICP: Ideal Customer Profile and Segments 2025+" (updated 27 Oct 2026, ICP and Positioning Lock v1.0 R11), plus "FW Customer Buying Triggers & Signals". Exports are in `raw/`. Where older docs disagree, the ICP doc wins (see ICP Notes).

**Language rule:** never write "asset-heavy" in outbound copy. It is an internal scoring term only.

## Target Profile

| Field | Value |

| Company size | **Outbound = Core only: 500+ employees (client decision 2026-10-29), revenue roughly $200M+. Sweet spot $1B to $20B.** Employee count is the lead filter, revenue confirms scale |
| Industries (Tier 1, source outbound here) | 1. Manufacturing and Industrials (machinery, equipment makers, industrial automation, conglomerates). 2. Energy (transition, grid, renewables, power; exclude upstream oil and gas). 3. Health and Life Sciences (medical devices, health tech, pharma manufacturing; de-emphasize providers and payers). 4. Logistics and Transportation (trucking, rail, freight, warehousing, 3PL). 5. Consumer Products (makers of goods incl. food production and packaging) |

Mexican operations of US/global parents qualify via the parent rule, but Mexico is not a target HQ country. **European-only accounts (no US or Canadian entity) are disqualified. ## Qualified Lead Definition

All six or the account goes to nurture, not pipeline:
1. **Named buyer:** CIO tier, CSCO, Chief AI Officer, Chief Innovation or Transformation Officer, or COO. Reports to the CEO or one level below.
2. **P&L problem:** buyer can name a specific number to move (margin points, freight cost, OTIF, working capital, cycle time, exception cost).
3. **Executive sponsor:** a C-level willing to sign for the outcome, not just approve budget.
4. **Operator champion:** logic owner, head of program or senior director who runs day-to-day delivery.
5. **Budget posture:** discretionary transformation budget allocated, or a workstream pulled from an incumbent SI's budget line.
6. **Commercial fit:** willing to entertain an outcome-staked SOW with a CFO-validatable value method.

## Signal Priority

Score an account higher with 2+ signals in a 90-day window. Signals decay in 60 to 180 days, so a 6-month-old signal is cold. The client is uploading a lead list, so signals are used to prioritize and personalize, not to detect.

| Signal | Priority | Why it works |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| New CIO, Chief AI Officer, CSCO or CFO in seat (under 12 months, strongest under 90 days) | 1 | New exec decides vendors inside the first two quarters |
| AI program failure, write-off or quiet reset | 2 | Budget exists, trust in the old approach is gone |
| Incumbent SI contract at renewal (Accenture, Wipro, TCS, Infosys, Deloitte, EY, Capgemini) | 3 | Moment to compare outcome-staked fees against time and materials |
| Board mandate on transformation ROI or AI operationalization | 4 | Top-down pressure for measured value |
| Supply chain disruption (recall, freight blowout, allocation miss, S&OP failure) | 5 | Operational pain with a P&L number attached |
| Restructuring, spin-off, IPO prep, activist pressure, post-M&A integration | 6 | Operational narrative must hold up |
| Nearshoring or footprint reconfiguration into Mexico by a US/global parent | 7 | Active operational change |
| Exec publishing on agentic AI, "pilot to production", transformation ROI or SI dissatisfaction | 8 | Self-identified and frustrated |
| Post-conference activity (Gartner SCM Symposium, ASCM Connect, CIO summits) | 9 | Actively shopping |

## Avoid List

| Exclusion | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| Under 200 employees or under $100M revenue, startups, Series A-C | Out of scope |
| Edge band (201-500 employees, $100M-$250M) | Inbound only |
| European-only accounts | Standing disqualifier |
| Mexico as HQ country | Only via a US/global parent |
| Metals and mining, upstream oil and gas | Capex cadence fights a 12-week motion |
| Private equity, funds, investment banks, asset managers | Inbound only by standing rule |
| Standalone defense | Watchlist only |
| Paper and packaging as a standalone lens | Sits inside Manufacturing and Consumer Products |
| Emerging-tech lens (quantum, web3, AR/VR, blockchain, gene therapy) | Not a primary lens |
| Marketing buyers (CMO, marketing ops) | Not in the buyer set |
| Staff augmentation, body-shop, time-and-materials requests | Future Works does not sell hourly capacity |
| Pilot-for-pilot's-sake with no production path | No Finance-validated value target |
| Accounts that will not let the CFO's office validate the baseline | Breaks the model |
| No named exec owner for AI or transformation; "we need a platform" tooling language | Disqualifying signals |
| No P&L problem big enough for a ~$400K cycle | Value math fails |

## Decision-Maker Notes

| Persona | Titles | P&L problem | What sells them |
|---------|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Gamora-CIO (primary) | CIO, CDO, CIDO, operational CTO, Chief Digital and Transformation Officer, Global Head of IT and Process Excellence | AI stuck in pilot, SI workstream underperforming, board narrative | Lower risk than the current integrator: named experts, sandbox first, fees staked on results, built on your stack |
| Gamora-SC (primary) | Chief Supply Chain Officer, EVP/SVP Supply Chain | Margin, freight, OTIF, working capital, allocation | Margin, freight, inventory and working capital moved in a quarter, verified by Finance |
| Gamora-AI | Chief AI Officer, Head of AI, VP AI, Head of Data and AI | 74% AI failure rate, pilot-to-production pressure | AI in production where it changes the P&L, not another pilot |
| Gamora-COO | COO, EVP/SVP Operations | Missed operational KPIs, slow SI mobilization | 12-week delivery against operational KPIs, service credit safety net |
| Gamora-DX (front door, not the closer) | Chief Innovation/Transformation Officer, Head of Innovation | Innovation theater fatigue | Sub-12-week phases, board-ready proof. Commercial close usually moves to CIO or CSCO |
| Influencers (can block) | CEO, CFO, Procurement | Board narrative, value validation | Risk absorbed by Future Works. A third of the fee contingent on value Finance validates |
| Champion (make or break) | Logic owner, head of program, senior director ops/IT | Day-to-day delivery | Pod runs delivery with them |
