# Voice — Future Works

How to write on behalf of Future Works. Read before drafting any copy. Source: "FW Positioning ... (Vendors)" voice rules, "FW Elevator Pitches", "FW Current Lead Magnets", future.works.

**Spelling: American English (US).** Client confirmed.

**Preferred brand lines (client-supplied):**
1. "Future Works is the AI-native transformation engine." (no audience noun after it)
2. "AI transformation with skin in the game."

---

## Brand Voice

**Overall tone:** Confident, plain-spoken, specific, expertise-forward.
**Personality:** Sharp operator explaining something, not a billboard. Direct, accountable, speed-focused.
**What to avoid:** Hype, consultant-speak, buzzword stacks, pitchy superlatives, anything that sounds like a platform sales page.

---

## Writing Rules

- Lead with the buyer's problem, quantify the outcome, name the mechanism.
- Anchor every claim to a proof point. If you cannot cite one, do not make the claim.
- **No em dashes.** Use commas, periods, colons or parentheses.
- No "not just X, it is Y" construction. No hollow superlatives.
- Company name is "Future Works", two words, always.
- Describe the technology, never name it: "our proprietary AI-native operating system", "proprietary agent orchestration", "our value architecture framework".
- No prices, percentage comparisons or the word "cheaper". Do not quote the tranche split percentages or the 15% bonus.
- No named competitor comparisons. Say "your current integrator" or "incumbent integrators".
- Keep figures out of the first cold email. Proof lines only where the sequence allows.
- Do not say "asset-heavy". Describe the operations instead.
- Short sentences, active voice, numbers as digits (this OS default, client copy uses "twelve-week" as a compound adjective in brand lines, so match the approved lines).

## Words to Avoid

| Avoid | Replace with |
|-------|-------------|
| asset-heavy operators / enterprises | Name the industries or the value. No catch-all noun |
| "AI-powered" with no specifics, "revolutionary", "game-changing", "best-in-class", "disrupt", "synergy" | A specific claim with a proof point |
| "leverage" (verb) | "use" |
| digital transformation | "AI-native transformation" |
| web design, app development, UI UX, digital product, innovation strategy, future of AI | Retired terms, do not use |
| "ROI or We Pay", "Free Until Value Pilot", "Twelve-Month Hypercare" | Retired commitments |
| "Enterprise AI Operationalization", "Agentic Execution of Enterprise Workflows" | "AI Operationalization", "Agentic Workflow Execution" |
| "cheaper", any price or percentage comparison | Outcome and risk language |
| SOC 2 / ISO 27001 as held certifications | "audits in progress and on schedule" |

## Approved Claims

Only these. Check the offer file for the exact approved wording and the conflicts still open.

- AI transformation with skin in the game.
- Delivers in twelve weeks what incumbents quote at two years.
- A third of every fee is staked on a value target the client's Finance team validates. Service credit if missed, bonus if exceeded.
- Named senior experts, no labor pyramid, no swap-outs after kickoff.
- A global health technology company: 3x return in twelve weeks, Finance-signed, funded the next cycle (Philips, anonymous).
- A global energy equipment manufacturer: more than 20,000 work hours a year removed in cycle one, every cycle extended since (NextPower, anonymous). 75% faster time to market.
- A two-year AI roadmap unlocked in four weeks (JLL).
- Staples: twenty digital products in twelve weeks.
- 74% of enterprises show no measurable value from AI investment (BCG 2024 AI at Work).
- GDPR-compliant delivery. SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 audits in progress.

**Do not use** "3.3x faster time-to-value" or "96% project success" (website, self-reported).

## Examples

### Good example (approved, Future Works's own ready-made copy)
> Future Works is the AI-native transformation engine. We rebuild core operations in twelve-week cycles, run by named senior experts orchestrating fleets of AI agents, with a third of every fee staked on value your Finance team validates.

### Good example (approved 1-minute pitch opener)
> Most AI dies in pilots. Most transformation programs run for two years before anyone sees value on the P&L. We fix both.

### Bad example
> Future Works leverages cutting-edge AI-powered solutions to revolutionize asset-heavy enterprises, offering best-in-class digital transformation at a fraction of consultant prices.
Why: "leverages", "cutting-edge", "AI-powered", "revolutionize", "asset-heavy", "best-in-class", "digital transformation" and a price comparison all break the rules above.
