## What They Sell

**Product / service:** Outcome-staked AI transformation cycles
**Category:** AI-native transformation (not digital transformation, not a systems integrator, not an AI vendor)
**Delivery model:** 12-week cycles run by a pod of named senior experts (Lead Architect, Principal Engineer, Data Steward, Outcome Owner), each orchestrating a fleet of AI agents on a proprietary AI-native operating system. No labor pyramid, no swap-outs after kickoff.
**Pricing:** Internal only, never in copy. Cycle 1 about $400K, Cycle 2 about $650K, multi-cycle programs in the low millions. Cycles 2 and 3 activate by email against a published rate card.

### Four capabilities (describe by value first, industry second)
1. **AI Operationalization:** AI strategy converted into a value-accounted use-case portfolio, with dollars per use case, visible to the CFO.
2. **Supply Chain and Fulfillment Transformation:** allocation, freight discipline, SKU rationalization, working capital release.
3. **Agentic Workflow Execution:** order-to-cash, procure-to-pay, S&OP, quote-to-cash, contracts, service operations, run by agents. Lead with revenue and value lift, not only cost.
4. **Operational Resilience and Working Capital:** margin and freight protection, inventory reduction, a named operating-cost line taken out.

Do not put "Enterprise" in front of capability names.

## The Outcome Promise

**Big claim:** Value realized in a quarter. The outcome lands on the P&L in 12 weeks, verified against a baseline the client's Finance team signs.
**Timeframe:** 12 weeks. Weeks 0-2 mobilize and lock the baseline, weeks 2-9 build and deploy (sandbox first), weeks 10-12 verify value with Finance.
**Guarantee / risk reversal (the guarantee is "outcome-staked fee"):** Every cycle fee splits into three equal tranches: mobilization, build and deploy, value verification. The third tranche is released only on Finance-validated value against a CFO Approved Baseline locked in week 2.
- Below target: tranche 3 converts to a service credit on the next phase (90-day expiry).
- At target: full fee confirmed.
- Above target: Future Works earns a 15% Overdrive Bonus on value above target.

**Retired, never use:** "ROI or We Pay", "Free Until Value Pilot", "Twelve-Month Hypercare".
**Percentages and rate-card detail stay out of public surfaces.** In cold email, say "a third of every fee is staked on value your Finance team validates". Do not quote the 15%, the tranche percentages or any price.

### Seven commitments
Named Senior Experts. Value-Based Fees. Sub-12-Week Phases. Change-Order-Free Sprints. Transparent Resource Plan. Vendor-Agnostic Architecture. Auditable by Design.

### Trust frame
Secure. Composable. Auditable. GDPR-compliant delivery. SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 are **audits in progress, never held certifications**. Built on AWS, Azure and Databricks.

## Key Differentiators

1. **Outcome-staked fees:** a third of every cycle fee rides on value Finance validates. Service credit if missed, bonus if exceeded. A time-and-materials model structurally cannot do this.
2. **AI agents plus named senior experts:** a small pod of named experts, each running a fleet of agents. No pyramid, no swap-outs.
3. **Pace:** twelve weeks vs the two years incumbents quote, with value shipped every cycle. A program is a sequence of cycles, not a multi-year commitment.
